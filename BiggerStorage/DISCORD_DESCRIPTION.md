# Bigger Storage 1.0.0
**Download:** https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/113

Chests full again? Bigger Storage gives every chest more slots and, if you want, lets your items stack much higher, so you spend less time building chests and sorting stacks.

### Features
- **Extra chest slots:** 0 to 100 (default 10) on top of the normal size, also for chests you already built. Conveyor chests, fuel storages and wells are left alone.
- **Stack size:** 0 to 300 in steps of 10 (default 0 = normal) in every inventory: yours, chests and stations. Items that do not stack (like tools) stay single.
- **Prepare for uninstall:** brings chests and stacks back to normal before you remove the mod. A message on screen tells you when you are done.

### Good to know
- **Changes your save game:** the chest size is saved with each chest. Before removing the mod, turn on *Prepare for uninstall*, take items out of the chests it tells you about and sleep once so the game saves. Bigger stacks you already have stay, the game handles them fine.
- Lowering the extra slots never shrinks a chest below the slots in use, so nothing gets lost.
- Settings in game via **F1** (ConfigurationManager) or **Mods** in the main menu or pause menu (GK2 Mod Framework). Changes apply instantly.
- Other mods that change chest or stack sizes may conflict.
