BIGGER STORAGE: Changelog
=========================

1.1.0 (2026-09-27)
------------------
- New option "Extra slots for wood, stone and marble" (0 to 100, default 10): the storages you
  put logs, stone blocks and marble blocks into (e.g. the wood storage at the sawmill) get more
  slots too, also the ones you already built. "Prepare for uninstall" resets them as well.
- New option "Show fill level" (on by default): a small bubble above these storages shows how full
  they are, e.g. 12/20, like the one above fuel storages.
- Checked against the game update 1.0.0.7 and GK2 Mod Framework 0.1.14: no changes needed. With
  0.1.12 or newer you can also change the settings in game via the Mods button in the pause menu.
- Reworded some texts.

1.0.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
Initial release:
- Extra chest slots: every chest gets up to 100 more slots, also the ones you already built.
- Stack size: up to 300 items per slot in all inventories (off by default).
- Prepare for uninstall: resets chests and stacks to normal before you remove the mod and tells you
  which chests are still too full.
- Settings in game via F1 (ConfigurationManager) or the GK2 Mod Framework menu, translatable
  (English and German included).
