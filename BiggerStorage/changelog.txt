BIGGER STORAGE: Changelog
=========================

1.0.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
Initial release:
- Extra chest slots: every chest gets up to 100 more slots, also the ones you already built.
- Stack size: up to 300 items per slot in all inventories (off by default).
- Prepare for uninstall: resets chests and stacks to normal before you remove the mod and tells you
  which chests are still too full.
- Settings in game via F1 (ConfigurationManager) or the GK2 Mod Framework menu, translatable
  (English and German included).
