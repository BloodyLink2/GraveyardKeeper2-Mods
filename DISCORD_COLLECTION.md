# Graveyard Keeper 2 Mods by BloodyLink2
Six quality-of-life mods for Graveyard Keeper 2. Each works on its own, and they are made to work well together. All settings are in game via **F1** or the **Mods** menu (GK2 Mod Framework), in English and German.

### Automation and storage
- **[Graveyard Automation](<https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/89>)**: finished items go straight into a nearby chest, ovens refuel themselves and keep crafting, zombies take ingredients from chests.
- **[Bigger Storage](<https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/113>)**: up to 100 extra slots for every chest and optional stacks of up to 300.
- **[Zombie Productivity](<https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/90>)**: zombies work up to 10x faster at workbenches, on conveyors and in the garden, with optional extra mastery.

### Your keeper and the week
- **[Tireless Keeper](<https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/79>)**: health and energy regenerate, insanity fades and sleep deprivation builds up slower. Optional infinite health and energy.
- **[Sermon Every Day](<https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/83>)**: hold sermons on any day of the week and as often as you like.
- **[Resurrect Every Day](<https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/87>)**: resurrect zombies on any day of the week and as often as you like.

### Better together
- **Graveyard Automation + Bigger Storage:** more room in the chests next to your stations, so they run longer on their own.
- **Graveyard Automation + Zombie Productivity:** faster zombies whose items go straight into a chest.
- **Resurrect Every Day + Zombie Productivity:** more zombie workers, and faster ones.

### Good to know
- Only Bigger Storage changes your save game (chest sizes). Turn on its *Prepare for uninstall* option before you remove it. The other mods store nothing and can be added or removed at any time.
- All mods need BepInEx 5 (x64). Details are on each Nexus page.
