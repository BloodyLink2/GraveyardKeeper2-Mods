# Sermon Every Day 1.2.0
**Download:** https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/83

In vanilla you can only preach once a week, on the Day of Wrath. Miss it, and you wait six days for your next chance to earn Faith. Sermon Every Day lets you hold sermons on any day of the week and as often as you like.

### Features
- **Sermons on any day:** on all six days of the week, not just the Day of Wrath. (On by default.)
- **Sermons per day:** a daily limit from 1 to 10, or 0 for unlimited (default). The limit also applies on the Day of Wrath.
- **Vanilla costs stay:** every sermon still consumes Gratitude equal to the number of parishioners.
- **Ignore Gratitude** (optional, off by default): the number of parishioners depends on church quality alone and no Gratitude is consumed.

### Good to know
- Settings in game via **F1** (ConfigurationManager) or **Mods** in the main menu or pause menu (GK2 Mod Framework). Changes apply instantly.
- Changes no save game values, so you can add or remove it at any time. The daily counter starts over after loading a save.
- Other mods that change when sermons are available may conflict.
