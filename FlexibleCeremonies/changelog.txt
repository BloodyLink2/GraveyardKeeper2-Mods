SERMON EVERY DAY: Changelog
===========================

1.2.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
- Translations: the settings (F1 menu and GK2 Mod Framework menu) and the mod description can now
  be translated with language files of the GK2 Mod Framework (0.1.8 or newer). English and German
  are included.
- Checked against the game update 1.0.0.5: no changes needed.

1.1.0 (2026-09-23)
------------------
- New option "Ignore Gratitude" (off by default): the number of parishioners depends on church
  quality alone and sermons no longer consume Gratitude.

1.0.0 (2026-09-23)
------------------
Initial release:
- Hold sermons on any day of the week, not just on the Day of Wrath.
- Sermons per day: limit per in-game day (0 = unlimited).
- Settings in game via F1 (ConfigurationManager) or the GK2 Mod Framework menu.
