# Graveyard Automation 1.8.1
**Download:** https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/89

No more running from oven to oven. Put a chest next to your stations and let them work: finished items go straight into the chest, ovens refuel themselves and keep crafting, and zombies no longer wait for you.

### Features
- **Zombie workbenches:** finished items go into a nearby chest and the zombie keeps working. Zombies also take their ingredients from nearby storages, optionally from chests in all other areas, so no hauler is needed.
- **Auto-crafting stations:** ovens, distillers, compost heaps and the like are collected automatically and start their next craft, even while you are elsewhere. Their research orbs fly straight to you.
- **Wells and beehives:** well water goes into a chest, full beehives are harvested into one, so both keep producing.
- **Refuel from chests:** the fuel storage makes exactly the fuel a waiting station needs from materials in your chests.
- **Repeat recipes:** a recipe queued once is set to 999x.
- **Pick recipes from nearby chests** (optional): Stardew style. Idle ovens and zombie workbenches craft whatever the ingredients in the chests next to them allow and share the work between them.
- **Also:** station speed up to 10x, one switch per kind of station, adjustable chest search radius, optional drop on the ground when no chest has room.

### Good to know
- No chest in range or chest full: the station works as in vanilla. Only real chests are used, never conveyor chests.
- Settings in game via **F1** (ConfigurationManager) or **Mods** in the main menu or pause menu (GK2 Mod Framework), in English, German and Russian.
- Stores no data in your save game, so you can add or remove it at any time.
- Other mods that change crafting output, the craft queue or station speed may conflict.
