GRAVEYARD AUTOMATION: Changelog
===============================

1.7.0 (2026-09-26)
------------------
- New option "Harvest beehives" (on by default): full beehives are harvested automatically, as if
  you did it yourself. The honey and beeswax go into a chest within the search radius, and the
  hive starts filling up again. Beehives also get their own switch under "Automated stations".
  Wild honey nests are left alone.
- Checked against the game patch 1.006 and GK2 Mod Framework 0.1.12: no changes needed. With
  0.1.12 you can also change the settings in game via the Mods button in the pause menu.
- Reworded some texts.

1.6.1 (2026-09-25)
------------------
- Fixed: when only part of a station's output fit into the chest, the rest could get lost; and
  extra items coming out of a finished craft could drop on the ground even with "Drop on the
  ground if no chest" off. The rest now goes into other chests within the search radius, then
  into any chest in the area; only if no chest has room at all does it drop, and the log says so.

1.6.0 (2026-09-25)
------------------
- New: one switch per kind of automated station ("Automated stations" in F1, "Stations" in the
  Mods menu). After loading a save, every kind of station the mod automates there is listed (ovens,
  compost heaps, wells, workbenches with a zombie worker and so on). Turn one off and the mod leaves
  that kind of station completely alone. All on by default.

1.5.0 (2026-09-25)
------------------
- New option "Pick recipes for zombie workbenches" (off by default): an idle zombie picks a recipe
  it can make (right tool and enough mastery) from the ingredients in nearby storages, crafts it
  once and then picks again. Recipes no other station in the area is making come first,
  then the one whose result you have the least of.

1.4.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
- New option "Zombie ingredients from chests" (on by default): zombie workers take the
  ingredients they need straight from the storages within the search radius, so no other zombie has
  to bring them anymore. Ingredients another zombie is already bringing are left to it; if there
  is not enough nearby, the zombie waits as usual.

1.3.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
- "Pick recipes from nearby chests" now crafts one at a time and checks all unlocked recipes again
  after each one: stations prefer recipes that no other station in the area is making right now,
  then the one whose result you have the least of. Several ovens share the work and your stock
  stays balanced. The next recipe is picked right after the finished item has been collected.
- The description of "Station speed" now says clearly that tables you work at yourself
  (workbenches, autopsy and embalming table) are not affected. Tireless Keeper can speed up your
  own work there.

1.2.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
- Translations: the settings (F1 menu and GK2 Mod Framework menu) and the mod description can now
  be translated with language files of the GK2 Mod Framework (0.1.8 or newer). English and German
  are included.

1.1.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
- New option "Collect research points" (on by default): the research points of stations the mod
  collects for you fly straight to you, like when you sleep, instead of dropping next to the
  station.
- New option "Empty wells into chests" (on by default): the water of upgraded wells is moved into
  a nearby chest, so the well never stops producing.
- Fixed: finished items could end up in the upgraded well (or other objects with storage that are
  not chests) when it was empty and there was no chest nearby. Only real chests are used now.
- Checked against the game update 1.0.0.5: no other changes needed.

1.0.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
Initial release:
- Zombie workbenches: finished items go straight into a nearby chest, the zombie keeps working.
- Auto-crafting stations (e.g. ovens): finished items are collected into a nearby chest and the
  station keeps running, even while you are somewhere else. Research points drop next to the
  station.
- Refuel from chests: fuel storages make the fuel a waiting station needs from materials in your
  chests.
- Repeat recipes: recipes queued with count 1 at ovens and zombie workbenches become 999.
- Pick recipes from nearby chests (off by default): idle ovens queue a recipe themselves when its
  ingredients are in a nearby chest.
- Station speed: ovens, distillers and the like work up to 10x faster.
- Chest search radius and optional "Drop on the ground if no chest".
- Settings in game via F1 (ConfigurationManager) or the GK2 Mod Framework menu.
