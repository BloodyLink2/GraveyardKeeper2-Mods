# Tireless Keeper 1.3.0
**Download:** https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/79

Tired of running out of energy halfway through your to-do list? Health and energy regenerate over time, insanity slowly fades and sleep deprivation builds up slower. Everything is adjustable, from a gentle quality-of-life boost to full god mode.

### Features
- **Health regeneration:** 0.5 HP per second by default (0 to 10), also during combat.
- **Energy regeneration:** 0.2 per second by default (0 to 5).
- **Insanity fades:** 0.1 per second by default (0 to 5), so your full energy bar comes back over time.
- **Slower sleep deprivation:** the debuff kicks in after 4 days instead of 2 by default (0 = never).
- **Sleep with full energy:** the bed no longer refuses, so you can save whenever you want. (On by default.)
- **Infinite health** and **infinite energy:** optional, off by default.
- **Your work speed at tables:** up to 10x more progress per hit at workbenches, the autopsy table and the like. (1.0 = normal by default.)
- **Combat:** stamina refills up to 10x faster or is infinite, and your zombies heal during fights (up to 10% of their health per second). Off by default.

### Good to know
- Settings in game via **F1** (ConfigurationManager) or **Mods** in the main menu or pause menu (GK2 Mod Framework). Changes apply instantly.
- Stores no data in your save game, so you can add or remove it at any time.
- Other mods that change player health, energy, insanity, sleep or combat values may conflict.
