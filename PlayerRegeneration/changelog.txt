TIRELESS KEEPER: Changelog
==========================

1.2.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
- New option "Your work speed at tables" (1.0 = normal, up to 10x): more progress per hit when you
  work at a table yourself, such as workbenches, the autopsy and embalming table or the study
  table. Every hit still costs energy as usual.

1.1.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
- Translations: the settings (F1 menu and GK2 Mod Framework menu) and the mod description can now
  be translated with language files of the GK2 Mod Framework (0.1.8 or newer). English and German
  are included.
- Checked against the game update 1.0.0.5: no changes needed.

1.0.0 (2026-09-23)
------------------
Initial release:
- Health regenerates automatically (adjustable, also during combat).
- Energy regenerates automatically (adjustable).
- Insanity fades automatically (adjustable).
- Sleep deprivation builds up slower (adjustable, 0 = never).
- Optional infinite health and infinite energy.
- Sleep with full energy: the bed no longer refuses when your energy bar is full, so you can always
  save.
- Settings in game via F1 (ConfigurationManager) or the GK2 Mod Framework menu.
