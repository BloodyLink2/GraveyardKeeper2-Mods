# Resurrect Every Day 1.1.0
**Download:** https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/87

In vanilla the power switch in the resurrection lab only works on the Day of Envy, and its storm power recharges just once a week. Prepared a table on the wrong day? Then your corpse waits until next week. Resurrect Every Day lets you pull the switch whenever you like.

### Features
- **Resurrect on any day:** the power switch works on all six days of the week. (On by default.)
- **Unlimited storm power:** the lab always has power, so you can resurrect as often as you like. Turn it off for the vanilla weekly recharge. (On by default.)
- **Nothing else changes:** you still prepare the tables with a body, all main organs, the resurrection liquid and a collar, then pull the switch as usual.

### Good to know
- Settings in game via **F1** (ConfigurationManager) or **Mods** in the main menu or pause menu (GK2 Mod Framework). Changes apply instantly.
- Stores no data in your save game, and with unlimited power on your weekly vanilla power is left untouched. You can add or remove it at any time.
- Other mods that change the resurrection lab or its power switch may conflict.
