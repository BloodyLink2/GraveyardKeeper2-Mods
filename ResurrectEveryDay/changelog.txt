RESURRECT EVERY DAY: Changelog
==============================

1.1.0 (2026-09-24)
------------------
- Translations: the settings (F1 menu and GK2 Mod Framework menu) and the mod description can now
  be translated with language files of the GK2 Mod Framework (0.1.8 or newer). English and German
  are included.
- Checked against the game update 1.0.0.5: no changes needed.

1.0.0 (2026-09-23)
------------------
Initial release:
- Resurrect zombies on any day of the week, not just on the Day of Envy.
- Unlimited storm power: the power switch in the resurrection lab always has power, so you can
  resurrect as often as you like. Your weekly vanilla power is left untouched.
- Settings in game via F1 (ConfigurationManager) or the GK2 Mod Framework menu.
