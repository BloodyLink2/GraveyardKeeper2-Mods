# Zombie Productivity 1.1.0
**Download:** https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/90

Your zombies are loyal, but not exactly fast. Zombie Productivity lets you decide how quickly they work at workbenches, on conveyor belts and in the garden, and can give them extra mastery so they take on recipes above their level.

### Features
- **Work speed:** 0.1 to 10x (default 1.0 = normal) for zombies at workbenches, on conveyors and for gardeners. The game lets a zombie hit at most once per work check, so simply speeding up time stops at about 5x. This mod carries out the extra hits too, so up to 10x really works.
- **Mastery bonus:** 0 to 10 extra mastery levels for working zombies (default 0). Higher mastery unlocks recipes above their level and gives more progress per hit.

### Good to know
- Settings in game via **F1** (ConfigurationManager) or **Mods** in the main menu or pause menu (GK2 Mod Framework). Changes apply instantly.
- Stores no data in your save game, so you can add or remove it at any time.
- Faster zombies still wait for you to pick up the result. To have finished items go straight into a chest, combine it with [Graveyard Automation](<https://www.nexusmods.com/graveyardkeeper2/mods/89>).
- Other mods that change how fast zombies work or their mastery may conflict.
